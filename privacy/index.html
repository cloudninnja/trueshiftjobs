Privacy policy

What personal data TrueShift Jobs uses, and why. We keep it to a minimum.

This policy explains how TrueShift Jobs ("we", "us") handles personal data when you use trueshiftjobs.com. We aim to collect as little as possible.

Jobseekers browsing the site

  • No accounts and no tracking. You do not need to sign up to use the site. We do not use advertising trackers or tracking cookies.
  • Visitor statistics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It does not use cookies, does not track you across websites and does not build a profile of you. It records things like the page visited, the website you came from, your country and your type of device.
  • Saved jobs and filters stay on your device. If you save a job or choose filters, they are stored in your own browser (local storage) so the site remembers them. We never receive this information. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, to deliver it and keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Links to other sites. When you click "Apply", you leave our site and go to the employer's or job site's own advert, which has its own privacy policy.

Contact form and employer job posts

Our contact form and our employer job form run on Google Forms. If you use them, we receive what you type in, such as your message, name, email address and job details. We use this only to reply to you, to check and publish job adverts, and to deal with problems. Google processes this data for us under its own terms.

When an employer job is published, only the job details are shown on the site (such as the job title, company, location, pay and how to apply). The poster's name and email address are never published.

Job alert emails

If you choose to sign up for job emails, we collect your email address and the job types and areas you pick. We store them with MailerLite, the email service we use to send job emails, which processes them for us under its own terms. We first send you an email asking you to confirm, and we only send job emails once you have confirmed. We use your details only to send you new jobs from TrueShift Jobs. We never sell them or share them with employers or advertisers.

Every email will include a way to unsubscribe, and you can unsubscribe at any time on our unsubscribe page. You can also ask us to remove your email address at any time using our contact form, and we will delete it.

Our lawful basis

We rely on our legitimate interests in running a free job site, answering messages and checking job adverts, on your request when you ask us to publish a job, and on your consent for job alert emails. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

How long we keep data

We keep job alert email addresses until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove them. We keep contact messages for up to 12 months and employer posts for up to 12 months after the job closes, unless we need them longer to deal with a complaint or a legal requirement.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, and you can object to how we use it. To make a request, please use our contact form or email hello@trueshiftjobs.com and write "Privacy request". You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
