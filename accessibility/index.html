Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to find a job on TrueShift Jobs.

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we do

  • Plain, simple English and short sentences.
  • Text and buttons with strong colour contrast.
  • Pages that work on phones, tablets and computers, and with zoom up to 200%.
  • Buttons and filters that work with a keyboard and screen readers, with clear labels.
  • No pop-ups, no auto-playing media and no cookie banners.

Known limitations

  • Job adverts on other websites, which our "Apply" buttons link to, are outside our control and may be less accessible.
  • The London area map is also available as a borough list in the filters, for anyone who finds the map hard to use.
  • We have not yet had a formal independent accessibility audit.

Tell us about a problem

If something on the site is hard to use, please use our contact form or email hello@trueshiftjobs.com and tell us the page and the problem. We will try to fix it quickly.
