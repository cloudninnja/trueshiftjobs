Data sources

Where the jobs on TrueShift Jobs come from, and how we credit them.

Every job links to its original advert. We collect jobs every morning, keep only those in London posted in the last 30 days (apprenticeships stay until they close), show each job once, and filter out pay below the legal minimum and common scam wording.

Job feeds

  • Reed: vacancies from reed.co.uk, through the Reed Jobseeker API.
  • Adzuna: Jobs by Adzuna, through the Adzuna API.
  • NHS Jobs: public vacancies from jobs.nhs.uk.
  • Teaching Vacancies: school jobs from GOV.UK Teaching Vacancies, used under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

Jobs checked one by one

Each night we also look for jobs on employers' own career sites and on public job sites such as GOV.UK Find a job, Civil Service Jobs, council job sites and other job boards, and Claude, an AI assistant, checks each one against eight checks before it is marked "Checked". Jobs it cannot fully confirm are not shown.

Employer posts

Employers can post jobs directly through our free form. Each post is checked against our rules before it is published, and marked "Posted by employer".
