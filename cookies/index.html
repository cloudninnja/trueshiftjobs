Cookie policy

Short version: we do not use cookies.

TrueShift Jobs does not set any cookies, and we do not use advertising trackers. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which works without cookies and does not track you across websites.

Local storage

To make the site easier to use, your browser stores two small items in its local storage on your own device:

  • trueshift-saved: the jobs you choose to save.
  • trueshift-filters: your last filters, such as your area and the group you picked.
  • ts_alert: a note that you have signed up for job emails, so the sign-up box does not ask again.

These stay on your device and are never sent to us. You can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Fonts

The site's font is hosted on our own site, so loading a page does not contact any font service.

Other websites

Our contact and employer forms run on Google Forms, and our "Apply" links go to other job sites. Those sites may use their own cookies, as explained in their own cookie policies.
