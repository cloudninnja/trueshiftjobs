Parents returning to work

  • You can ask for flexible working, such as school hours or term time only, from your first day in a job.
  • Working parents in England may get up to 30 hours a week of funded childcare from 9 months old.
  • Tax-Free Childcare adds £2 for every £8 you pay in, up to £2,000 per child a year.
  • Check what you can get on GOV.UK.

Apprenticeships

  • An apprenticeship is a paid job with training towards a qualification. Level 3 is the same level as A levels, and Level 6 is degree level.
  • Apprentices get at least £8.00 an hour if under 19 or in the first year, then the minimum wage for their age.
  • You never pay for the training. Walk away from any "apprenticeship" that asks you for a fee.
  • Search all of England on GOV.UK Find an apprenticeship.

New to the UK

  • Employers must check your right to work before you start.
  • With an eVisa, get a right-to-work share code from GOV.UK. It starts with W and lasts 90 days.
  • Never send passport copies or bank details before a real interview.
  • Citizens Advice and your council's job service can help for free.

Free UK CV template

Not sure what a UK CV looks like? Pick the template closest to you, then copy it or download it for Word and fill in the parts in [square brackets].

UK CV basics

  • Keep it to one page for part-time jobs, two at most.
  • Leave out your photo, age, date of birth, marital status and nationality.
  • Put your most recent job or study first.
  • Say when you can work. Part-time employers look for this first.
  • Explain any gap in a line, such as a career break to raise children.
  • If you have a National Insurance number, say so, but don't write the number.

Stay safe while you apply

  • Never pay to get a job. Real employers do not charge for training, uniforms or checks.
  • Big names get copied. Official Amazon UK warehouse jobs are only on jobsatamazon.co.uk.
  • Be wary of messages out of the blue offering easy money by text, WhatsApp or Telegram.
  • Apply only through the link on the job. It takes you to the employer or a known job site.
