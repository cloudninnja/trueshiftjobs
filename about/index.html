About TrueShift Jobs

Why I built a free London job site that shows real, current jobs and leaves out the noise.

Why TrueShift Jobs exists

I'm Vamsi Are, an IT consultant based in London. TrueShift Jobs started at my own kitchen table, helping my daughter look for an apprenticeship.

What should have been exciting quickly became frustrating. The same vacancy appeared again and again through different agencies. Adverts stayed up long after the job had gone. Some "apprenticeships" turned out to be paid training courses, and some adverts paid less than the legal minimum. We spent hours searching and very little of that time went on jobs that were really there.

I realised that if it was this hard for us, it was harder still for many others: students looking for part-time work, parents going back to work after raising children, and people who have just moved to the UK and don't yet know what a genuine advert looks like, or that a real employer never asks you to pay.

I spend my working days building systems that automate tedious work for businesses, so I pointed those skills at this problem. TrueShift Jobs does the boring filtering for you.

What it does

  • Collects real, current London vacancies every morning from official job feeds. See Data sources.
  • Shows each job once, even when it appears on several job sites.
  • Removes adverts older than 30 days, scam wording, paid-course "jobs" and pay below the legal minimum. Apprenticeships stay until their closing date, because they are often advertised months ahead.
  • Sorts everything by borough, so you can find work close to home.
  • Groups jobs for students, people new to the UK, apprentices, graduates, parents returning to work and experienced professionals.
  • Lets employers post jobs for free. Every employer post is checked against our rules before it goes live.

What it is not

TrueShift Jobs is not an employer or a recruitment agency. We don't take applications, and we never charge jobseekers. When you apply, you apply on the employer's or job site's own advert.

It is free for jobseekers and always will be. If it saves you even one wasted application, it has done its job.
