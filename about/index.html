About TrueShift Jobs

A free London job site that shows real, current jobs and leaves out the noise.

Job hunting in London can be exhausting. The same vacancy appears ten times through different agencies, adverts stay up long after the job has gone, and some "jobs" turn out to be paid training courses or scams.

TrueShift Jobs was built to fix that. Every morning we collect real, current vacancies across London, remove duplicates, drop anything older than 30 days, filter out scam wording and pay below the legal minimum, and sort every job by borough.

Who it is for

We focus on people who are often poorly served by big job boards: students looking for part-time work, people new to the UK, school leavers looking for apprenticeships, graduates, parents returning to work, and experienced professionals who want a cleaner list.

How it works

  • Thousands of jobs come from official job feeds every night. See Data sources.
  • A smaller number are checked one by one against eight checks, such as a real employer, legal pay and no fees, and are marked "Checked".
  • Employers can post jobs for free. Every employer post is checked by a person before it goes live.

What we are not

TrueShift Jobs is not an employer or a recruitment agency. We do not take applications, and we never charge jobseekers. When you apply, you apply on the employer's or job site's own advert.
