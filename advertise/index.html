Post a job for free

Posting a vacancy on TrueShift Jobs is free. Post a job in about a minute: paste your advert, add your company name and email, and we do the rest. Every post is checked by a person before it goes live, and it appears on the right borough page and in the right groups, such as students or parents returning to work.

Published employer jobs get their own page on our site, marked up so search engines can show them in job searches.

Our standards

We only accept genuine vacancies that pay at least the UK minimum wage and never charge applicants. We do not accept adverts for paid courses presented as jobs, commission-only roles or schemes that ask jobseekers for money.
